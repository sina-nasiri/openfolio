====================================
🚀 GET STARTED IN 5 MINUTES
====================================

Step 1: Edit config.json
   • Update personal.name with your name
   • Update personal.email with your email
   • Update personal.title with your job title
   • Update social links

Step 2: Replace Images
   • assets/images/headshot.jpg - Your photo
   • assets/images/og-image.jpg - Social sharing image

Step 3: Test
   • Open index.html in your browser
   • OR run a local server (recommended)

Step 4: Deploy
   • Upload to your hosting via FTP
   • OR use free hosting (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel)

✅ Done! Your portfolio is live!
